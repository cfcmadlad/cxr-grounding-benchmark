## 2. Project

Title idea: neutral benchmark of vision-language and detection models for chest X-ray anatomical region grounding.

Research question: can models trained mainly for pathology or finding grounding also do anatomical region grounding, and how do they compare under one consistent, neutral evaluation protocol? Gap: the closest prior comparisons (RadVLM, AnatomiX, CheXOne papers) are self benchmarks by the authors.

Three core findings the supervisor wants the paper built around: (1) training data overlap, (2) left and right (laterality) confusion linked to architecture, (3) prompt sensitivity.

Dataset: Chest ImaGenome gold subset. The gold CSV has 1,000 frontal MIMIC-CXR images, 25,989 rows, 26 region names. We benchmark 959 images (40 image files missing on disk plus one truncated JPEG, `671f3a3e-221de8c3-0fa97b51-4ff046ae-39b82afa.jpg`, which fails to load; the Phase 1 "41 failures" are these). 15 core regions: right lung, left lung, cardiac silhouette, mediastinum, trachea, right and left upper lung zone, right and left mid lung zone, right and left lower lung zone, right and left hilar region, right and left costophrenic angle. 14,385 queries per model (959 x 15), 14,384 with a gold box (right lower lung zone is missing for one image). The gold CSV has exactly one row per (image, region), so no per-annotator data is inside it. Its `annotator` column holds combined labels such as `C&&W` and `S&&J` (assumed to be the initials of annotators whose boxes were reconciled).

Models (8): MAIRA-2, BioViL-T, CheXagent, Grounding DINO, BiomedParse, RadVLM, MedGemma 1.5 (4B), ChEX. Optional later: AnatomiX (arXiv 2601.03191, code and weights released, github.com/aneesurhashmi/anatomix).

Metrics: mean IoU in two forms, unconditional (missing or invalid prediction counts as 0, over all queries, the primary ranking metric) and conditional (valid boxes only, secondary). Also detection rate, recall at 0.25 and 0.5, signed mean centre offset (dx, dy) in normalised units, and mAP at 0.5 (see `evaluate.py: compute_map_at_05`). Mean IoU and mAP at 0.5 are not redundant; report both when relevant. Boxes are normalised by original width and height.
